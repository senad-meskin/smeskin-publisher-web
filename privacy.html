Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026.

This policy explains what data the smeskin website and the smeskin publisher application (the "App") handle. The short version: we do not collect personal data from visitors or viewers.

Website

This website is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no forms. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) as described in GitHub's privacy statement. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

The App

The App is a private tool used only by Senad Meškin, the owner of the smeskin accounts, to upload his own videos to his own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. When the owner signs in, the App receives:

  • access tokens for those accounts, issued by Google, Meta or TikTok through their official login (the App never sees a password);
  • basic account information needed to publish: account ID, username and display name;
  • for TikTok, the account's allowed posting options (for example privacy levels and maximum video length).

This data is used only to upload and publish videos and to show the result to the owner. Tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never sold, shared or sent anywhere other than the platform that issued them. The App does not access other users' data, followers' data, messages or comments.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner revokes access or deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time in the platform's settings (Google account permissions, Instagram apps and websites, TikTok app permissions), which makes the stored tokens useless.

Children

The App is not directed at children and does not collect data from anyone other than the account owner.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The effective date above will change when it is.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: send a direct message to @smeskin on Instagram.
